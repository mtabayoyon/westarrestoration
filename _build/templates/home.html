    How a job runs

    Every loss follows the same order, so you always know what's next.

    1. You call. We ask what happened, where, and whether it's still active, and give you steps to take before we arrive.
    2. We make it safe. Stop the source, extract water, tarp, board up, or contain, whichever the loss needs first.
    3. We dry and clean. Equipment runs and we log moisture readings until materials are back to normal.
    4. We document. Photos, readings, and a written scope your adjuster can work from.
    5. We rebuild, or hand off. Your choice of contractor for the repairs.
