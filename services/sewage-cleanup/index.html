Sewage cleanup

Toilet overflows and sewer backups. Category 3 water needs protective equipment, removal of porous materials, and disinfection.

Call (619) 365-4550, 24/7

Do this now

  1. Keep people and pets away from the area.
  2. Stop using toilets, sinks, and drains in the house.
  3. Don't run fans; they spread contaminants.
  4. Wash hands right away after any contact.

What we do

  1. Protect. Crews in protective equipment set up containment.
  2. Remove. Contaminated water and porous materials such as carpet, pad, and drywall come out.
  3. Disinfect. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobials.
  4. Dry and restore. The area is dried and rebuilt once it's clean.

Questions

Can carpet be saved after a sewage backup?

No. Carpet and pad touched by sewage are removed. Hard surfaces can be cleaned and disinfected.

Who pays for a sewer backup?

Many policies exclude it unless you have a sewer backup endorsement. If the city main caused it, a claim with the city may apply.
