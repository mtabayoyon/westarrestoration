Mold remediation

Visible growth or a musty smell. We contain the area, remove the mold, fix the moisture source, and clear the space for a post-test.

Call (619) 365-4550, 24/7

Do this now

  1. Don't touch, scrub, or bleach it; that releases spores.
  2. Keep the door to the affected room closed.
  3. Stop the moisture source if you can find it.
  4. Keep anyone with asthma or allergies away from the area.

What we do

  1. Assess. Find the extent of growth and the moisture that's feeding it.
  2. Contain. Plastic barriers and negative air pressure keep spores from spreading through the house.
  3. Remove. Affected drywall, insulation, and materials come out; framing is cleaned and treated.
  4. Verify. An independent post-remediation test confirms the area is clear.

Questions

Do I need a mold test first?

If growth is visible, testing isn't required to start. Testing helps when you smell mold but can't see it, and after remediation to confirm it's gone.

Is mold covered by insurance?

Mold from a covered water loss often is, up to a policy limit. Mold from long-term humidity usually isn't.
