Fire and smoke damage restoration

Soot, smoke odor, and water from firefighting. We secure the property, clean or remove what's damaged, and get rid of the smell for good.

Call (000) 000-0000, 24/7

Do this now

  1. Stay out until the fire department says the building is safe.
  2. Don't wipe soot; it smears and sets into surfaces.
  3. Don't turn on the HVAC; it spreads soot through the ducts.
  4. Call your insurer to open a claim.

What we do

  1. Secure. Board-up and roof tarping keep weather and intruders out.
  2. Clean soot. Dry sponging, HEPA vacuuming, and surface-specific cleaners for walls, ceilings, and contents.
  3. Remove odor. Thermal fogging, hydroxyl, or ozone treatment, depending on the materials.
  4. Restore. Repair or rebuild damaged areas, or hand off to our reconstruction crew.

Questions

Can smoke smell really be removed?

Yes, when the source residue is cleaned and the odor is neutralized rather than masked. Porous items that can't be cleaned are documented for replacement.

What about wildfire smoke?

Smoke from nearby wildfires gets into attics, HVAC, and soft goods even without flames. It's cleaned the same way and is often covered.
